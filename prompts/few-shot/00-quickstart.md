# few-shot prompts quickstart

**propose:** collect example demonstrations for few-shot and in-context learning.

**input:** curated input-output pairs, labelled examples.

**output:** few-shot pools that slot into prompt templates at inference time.

Related reusable prompts are in [inspection_agent.md](../system/inspection_agent.md), [inspection_paragraph.md](../templates/inspection_paragraph.md), and [visit_questions.md](../templates/visit_questions.md).
