# Risks and responsible use

Task T-VE-09: project operating note, pending team review. This document describes data handling and intended controls; it is not legal advice, a compliance certification or evidence of a deployed AI system. The team board references Egypt's Law 151/2020; legal interpretation and actual organizational requirements require the responsible custodian's review before real personal data is collected or sent to a provider.

## Intended use and human responsibility

SpaceEye supports inspection prioritization and training demonstrations for coded R7 projects. A person must review source evidence, uncertainty and alternative explanations before action. A priority level is not a finding of misconduct, legal distress, contractual breach or a verified fine. No automatic sanction or adverse action is justified by these outputs.

## Data minimization and access

Prepared exports use codes and mask planted telephone numbers. The pre-model baseline additionally handles Unicode phone formats, explicitly labelled personal-name fields and supplied name dictionaries. It masks accepted and rejected complaint text before export; its zero-detectable-identifiers assertion and regression checks do not establish exhaustive detection of every real name or identifier. Real free text needs an additional review before any model/provider receives it. Private mappings, raw defects, quarantines, answer keys and model checkpoints are excluded from the team bundle; their access remains limited to authorized maintainers.

Inspection contexts exclude private truth and separate generated sections. Before enabling external AI calls, confirm provider/model, budget, destination, access and retention settings, and the fields permitted to leave the environment. No external AI call, provider configuration or production access-control system is implemented by this note.

## Reliability and fairness limits

Satellite coverage, vegetation, roofs and source availability may affect projects differently. The present scope is only 12 R7 compounds and six public backtest cases. Missing evidence remains unknown rather than a negative finding. Annual height after 2024 is not an approved observation. The existing 2019Q2 data-event flag must remain visible.

Synthetic finance, complaints, visits and labels reflect declared generator assumptions. They do not demonstrate real-world accuracy or fairness. Current three-class training has no completed example. Historical binary performance at capacity 8 does not validate the current features or capacity 4. Complaint-template splits limit direct leakage, but generated wording is not representative evidence of actual users or dialects.

## Output review and correction

Future AI text must cite attached record IDs, preserve dates/units/codes and distinguish public evidence from assumptions. Numbers and claims need verification against source JSON, and unsupported causal claims need human review. The verifier/log and final Arabic HTML/PDF inspection files are still to be implemented. Until then, contexts are inputs, not verified reports.

Keep generated demonstrations labelled `Synthetic - for training only`. Do not use synthetic visits as field evidence, describe estimated height as a survey, or interpret fine assumptions as actual legal amounts. Record corrections through a reviewed version and preserve the previous accepted artifact. If an export exposes an identifier or an unsupported claim, stop sharing that export, notify its responsible custodian and issue a reviewed correction.

Responsibilities: data owner reviews provenance and masking; model/AI owner reviews leakage, claims and provider settings; dashboard owner preserves labels and uncertainty; the human committee owns operational decisions. Actual named acceptance and formal cross-review remain outstanding.
