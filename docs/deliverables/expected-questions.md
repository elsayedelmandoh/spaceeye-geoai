# Expected questions

Task T-SA-09. One-line answers for the presentation (spec section 12). Numbers come from repo files and are generated or real as marked. Codes only, no names. Draft for review.

| Question                                        | One-line answer                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Source                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Are the numbers real?                           | The priority list uses real public data with sources; the financial part is generated and labelled "Synthetic - for training only".                                                                              | `data/DATASET_CONTRACT.md`                 |
| Are you calling a developer distressed?         | No: we say "no building activity for N quarters" or "passed its first announced date by N months", and the committee decides.                                                                                    | `kpi-cards.md`                             |
| Why trust satellites?                           | The activity score was chosen over the simpler K1 check by the pre-registered tests T1-T3 (balanced accuracy +3.0 and +5.1 points; `data/K1_FALLBACK_CHECK.md`). The Dynamic World and Open Buildings correlation is not sourced in the repo yet, so no figure is quoted.                                                     | `docs/requirements/01-specs.md`                 |
| Why did the backtest fail?                      | The rule alerted early in 1 of 6 real cases because the five missed sites were seen as active: it detects stalls and passed dates, not pace against plan. The one hit (C21) came from a start date set by publication timing and a lag in the activity score, not from a real stall. These counts do not prove why each delay happened. | `data/BACKTEST_CURRENT.md`, `DIAGNOSIS.md` |
| Did you lower the success criterion?            | No: the criterion of 5 cases was fixed before computing and was not lowered; it was not achieved.                                                                                                                | `data/BACKTEST_CURRENT.md`                 |
| Only 96 training rows? Is this AI?              | It is a diagnostic on generated data (96 training and 48 test rows, 4 distressed test cases), so scores are not accuracy claims and we need ACUD's real data.                                                    | `data/EW_REPORT_CURRENT.md`                |
| Did adding ACUD-style data improve the warning? | Not clearly: with only 4 test cases, recall at 4 was 3 of 4 and 3 of 4 for the public and combined rules, 1 of 4 and 2 of 4 for logistic (satellite, combined) and 4 of 4 and 1 of 4 for boosting (satellite, combined), against a random-list baseline of 0.335 (2,000 draws), so the methods cannot be separated.                                                              | `data/EW_REPORT_CURRENT.md`                |
| Why no completion %?                            | Images see roofs, not plaster and ceramics; only supervisor reports show completion and interior finishing.                                                                                                      | `docs/requirements/01-specs.md`            |
| Why levels and not a score?                     | A 0-100 score would suggest precision that does not exist, so we use the worst of three signals with no weights.                                                                                                 | `kpi-cards.md`                             |
| Is the EGP value a real saving?                 | No: under our assumptions it ranges from 0.007 to 0.102 million EGP per year; the inputs are public proxies or assumptions.                                                                                            | `configs/cost_assumptions.yaml`            |
| Is K5 a fine?                                   | No: K5 counts months that would make a compound eligible for review under contract terms; it is illustrative and never sets a level.                                                                             | `kpi-cards.md`                             |
| Who decides which compounds are visited?        | The inspection committee; the system only ranks and gives a reason sentence.                                                                                                                                     | `docs/requirements/01-specs.md`            |
| Why R7 only and 12 compounds?                   | R7 is the approved scope, and the 12 coded R7 compounds are the ones with a reviewed boundary label from the location audit (no numeric confidence threshold is applied).                                                                                                              | `docs/requirements/01-specs.md`            |
| What do you need from ACUD?                     | Land allocation dates, contract fine rates, supervisor reports, field review records, escrow and installment data, and anonymised complaints.                                                                    | `docs/deliverables/data-request.md`        |
| Is the complaints model good? | Not yet: on the 104-row benchmark, the keyword baseline scored 0.27 and TF-IDF plus logistic scored 0.22; on the 30-item check set, they scored 0.37 and 0.30. The texts were written by the team and do not prove real accuracy, so it is shown only as a capability demo. | `docs/deliverables/complaints_eval.csv` |
| What happens today and what changes? | Today every compound gets the same field review every 3 months (public statement; 48 a year in R7); with the list, the committee visits the highest levels first and records every result (`as-is.md`, `to-be.md`). |
| Why now? | Decree 2184/2022 art. 5 applies to projects under way, and two R7 clocks run today: C18 is 57 months past its first announced date and C14 has moved its date twice (`decision-memo.md`). |
| How many buyers are affected? | The five late public cases hold 6,474 homes; about 153,816 home-months of waiting if all shared the first promised date, counted from the published promise, not the contract. |
| When does the system fail? | On sites that keep working but too slowly (C14, C12, C19) and on delays in finishing or handover (C10, C02); it reacts to stalls and passed dates, not pace (District view). |
| What does a false alarm cost against a missed case? | With the central assumptions one missed case costs as much as about 6.7 false alarms; no real false-alarm rate exists yet because all six backtest cases were late. |
| How do you start without risk? | One pilot quarter while the 3-month reviews continue, all 12 visit results recorded, proposed gates before each phase; going back costs at most one rerun (`transition-plan.md`). |
| Who is responsible if it goes wrong? | The committee decides every visit; the Track A owner runs and checks each quarter with a second reviewer; roles and risks are proposed in `governance.md`. |
| Can it work in another district? | Not proven: only R7 has been run; another district needs coded boundaries, dated statements and satellite windows, then the same checks (`knowledge-transfer.md`). |
