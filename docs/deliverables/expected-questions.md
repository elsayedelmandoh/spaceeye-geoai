# Expected questions

Task T-SA-09. One-line answers for the presentation (spec section 12). Numbers come from repo files and are generated or real as marked. Codes only, no names. Draft for review.

| Question                                        | One-line answer                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Source                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Are the numbers real?                           | The priority list uses real public data with sources; the financial part is generated and labelled "Synthetic - for training only".                                                                              | `data/DATASET_CONTRACT.md`                 |
| Are you calling a developer distressed?         | No: we say "no building activity for N quarters" or "passed its first announced date by N months", and the committee decides.                                                                                    | `kpi-cards.md`                             |
| Why trust satellites?                           | Dynamic World matches Open Buildings (r about 0.96 on recomputed values) and the activity score passed 5 pre-registered tests. [verify in the dataset audit]                                                     | `data/DATASET_CONTRACT.md`                 |
| Why did the backtest fail?                      | The rule alerted early in 1 of 6 real cases because the five missed sites were seen as active: it detects stalls and passed dates, not pace against plan, and these counts do not prove why each delay happened. | `data/BACKTEST_CURRENT.md`, `DIAGNOSIS.md` |
| Did you lower the success criterion?            | No: the criterion of 5 cases was fixed before computing and was not lowered; it was not achieved.                                                                                                                | `data/BACKTEST_CURRENT.md`                 |
| Only 96 training rows? Is this AI?              | It is a diagnostic on generated data (96 training and 48 test rows, 4 distressed test cases), so scores are not accuracy claims and we need ACUD's real data.                                                    | `data/EW_REPORT_CURRENT.md`                |
| Did adding ACUD-style data improve the warning? | Not clearly: with only 4 test cases, boosting got 4 of 4 on satellite features and 1 of 4 on combined features, so the methods cannot be separated.                                                              | `data/EW_REPORT_CURRENT.md`                |
| Why no completion %?                            | Images see roofs, not plaster and ceramics; only supervisor reports show completion and interior finishing.                                                                                                      | `docs/requirements/01-specs.md`            |
| Why levels and not a score?                     | A 0-100 score would suggest precision that does not exist, so we use the worst of three signals with no weights.                                                                                                 | `kpi-cards.md`                             |
| Is the EGP value a real saving?                 | No: under our assumptions it ranges from -0.006 to 0.098 million EGP per year, and every cost input is an assumption.                                                                                            | `configs/cost_assumptions.yaml`            |
| Is K5 a fine?                                   | No: K5 counts months that would make a compound eligible for review under contract terms; it is illustrative and never sets a level.                                                                             | `kpi-cards.md`                             |
| Who decides which compounds are visited?        | The inspection committee; the system only ranks and gives a reason sentence.                                                                                                                                     | `docs/requirements/01-specs.md`            |
| Why R7 only and 12 compounds?                   | R7 is the approved scope and only 12 compounds had boundaries confirmed at the required confidence.                                                                                                              | `docs/requirements/01-specs.md`            |
| What do you need from ACUD?                     | Land allocation dates, contract fine rates, supervisor reports, field review records, escrow and installment data, and anonymised complaints.                                                                    | `docs/deliverables/data-request.md`        |
